A DAILY-UPDATED PUBLIC RECORD

Pakistan Conflict & Incident Record

Tracking documented armed conflict between India and Pakistan since 1947, and incidents attributed to unidentified gunmen within Pakistan since 2020. Built from ACLED and public historical records; refreshed automatically.

Sources & methodology

History (1947–present): Hand-compiled list of major wars, standoffs, and cross-border strikes between India and Pakistan. Maintained manually — see data/india_pakistan_history.json.

Civilians / Militants killed by unknown gunmen: Aggregated by our own pipeline directly from named Pakistani and Indian news outlet RSS feeds (Dawn, Express Tribune, The News International, The Nation; The Hindu, Hindustan Times), plus Google News RSS as a supplementary net for coverage outside that curated list — filtered to articles mentioning unidentified or unknown gunmen in connection with Pakistan. No third-party conflict database is used. Records are individual news articles, not verified incidents — the same event is often covered by multiple articles across outlets. Classification into "civilian" vs "militant/terrorist" context is a headline keyword heuristic, not a verified legal determination.

Update cadence: A scheduled job re-runs the aggregator daily and appends anything not already recorded, de-duplicated by article URL.

Coverage window: RSS feeds — from any publisher, or from Google News — only ever expose a rolling window of recent items (days to a few weeks). This pipeline cannot retrieve articles from 2020–2023 today; the dataset builds forward in time from whenever backfill is first run. See the README for options if full 2020→ history is required.

This project documents publicly reported incidents for research and accountability purposes. It does not independently verify every underlying report.

Data refreshed automatically via GitHub Actions. Source code and raw JSON available in this repository.
